# Who we are
Refugee Inclusion (RI) works alongside refugees and forcibly displaced people to
remove barriers to inclusion, stability, and well-being. We center community voices and
lived experiences while creating opportunities for people and families to belong,
contribute, and thrive.

# What we do
We create culturally responsive, community-based programs that help refugees and
forcibly displaced people overcome barriers, strengthen connections, and build
pathways toward stability, inclusion, and well-being.
